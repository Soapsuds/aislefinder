Store map, top down — refined

Concept 06 from the aisles-and-pins exploration, carried forward: now four aisles, recolored to the 4N-e "Amber Nav" scheme from mockups/color-schemes.html. The route sweeps diagonally across the store — in at the bottom-left, out at the pin in the top-right — reading as one confident trip across the map.

#153f6e navy deep #1f5fa0 navy #ffb52e amber (light) #d99a2b gold (dark)

The mark

Four aisle bars seen from above, alternating full and faded white so the store reads as rows with depth. The dashed amber route enters at the bottom-left and curves gently up across the aisles to the pin at the top-right — the whole trip in a single rising gesture.

Amber appears exactly twice — the route and the pin — matching Amber Nav's rule that amber marks "where you're going" against an otherwise all-navy structure.

Light and dark tiles

The light-scheme icon sits on deep navy with bright amber (#ffb52e). The dark-scheme variant follows the palette's guidance that pale amber washes out on dark grounds — the tile deepens to the dark body navy and the route/pin shift to gold (#d99a2b).

Light scheme — navy deep #153f6e + amber #ffb52e

Primary app icon. The tile navy matches the filled section headers in Amber Nav's light mode.

Dark scheme — body navy #0d1420 + gold #d99a2b

For dark-mode contexts (tinted iOS icons, dark splash). Bars soften to the dark palette's pale navy #96bee6.

Size ramp

The dashed route holds up to about 32px. Below that the smallest sizes swap to a simplified cut — same four bars, bigger pin, no route — so the favicon never turns to noise.

Lockups

Wordmark stays Arial per the one-family rule. "Aisle" takes the scheme navy, "Finder" the body text color — the same two-tone logic the app's bold/regular wordmark already uses.
