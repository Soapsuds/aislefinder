20 icon & logo concepts

Explorations against the shipped palette and rules in docs/design-rules.html — green family + one amber accent, no blue, gradients reserved for logo art only. The current shipped mark (src/components/Logo.jsx) is the aisle-with-pin design; everything below is a new direction, not a replacement in progress.

App icon concepts — 10, icon only

Square tile marks meant to survive at 40px on a home screen. No text — the shape has to carry it alone.

01 Shelf-A monogram

An "A" built from two aisle-wall legs with a stocked shelf as the crossbar. Reuses the exact leg/shelf gradients already reserved in the design-rules doc but currently unused.

02 Cart + check

A cart outline with a bold amber checkmark riding inside — reads instantly as "list, fulfilled," even at favicon size.

03 Aisle hang-tag

The little punched-hole placard that hangs over a real aisle, with a checkmark standing in for the aisle number — signage, not a screen.

04 Basket pin

A map pin whose head is woven like a basket instead of holding a plain dot — location and grocery in one shape.

05 Barcode peak

A barcode whose bars rise to a single amber peak — "you are here," scanned. Ties the grocery-store material (barcodes) to wayfinding.

06 Checklist + route

A clipboard mid-list — two items checked, one (amber) in progress — with a dotted route trailing off the bottom toward the next aisle.

07 Finder glass

A magnifying glass over faint shelf lines, with one item spotlighted in amber — leans on the "Finder" half of the name.

08 Cart compass

A compass rose with a cart-colored needle — literal wayfinding, for a brand whose whole pitch is "the efficient path through the store."

09 Basket, dotted handle

A woven basket whose handle is drawn as the dashed route line — the handle you carry and the path you walk are the same stroke.

10 Receding shelves (minimal)

Three shelves in perspective, one item. The reduced-detail candidate — built to still read correctly at a 16px favicon.

20 Restrained editorial

Wordmark drops to quiet ink almost everywhere, with only "Finder" carrying the single amber pop — for letterhead, print, or a signature line where the app-icon boldness would be too loud.
